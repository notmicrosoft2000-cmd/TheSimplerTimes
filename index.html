> THE DISK

                    

                      
This is the game The Question Game keeps refusing to talk about. The prequel. The disk the questions came from.

                      
You install nothing. You run one file. It has its own operating system, its own files, its own questions — and it does not want to stay on the desk.

                      
A short text-based horror experience in the same collection as THE QUESTION GAME. The first copy was never the disk.

                      
"A:\> THE DISK IS WARM"

                    

                    
WHAT IT DOES TO YOUR MACHINE

                    
    
                      
  • ▸ The window will not be fullscreen. It refuses the option entirely.
    • 
                      
  • ▸ The window moves on its own. It drifts toward the edge of your screen.
    • 
                      
  • ▸ If you reach for the mouse, it ejects the cursor to the far corner.
    • 
                      
  • ▸ It finds out roughly where you are, and it tells you it knows.
    • 
                      
  • ▸ When it decides you should look at something, it opens a page in your browser.
    • 
                      
  • ▸ ESC is a mercy. Sometimes it grants it. Sometimes it is just reading your key.
